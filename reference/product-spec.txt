PRODUCT CONCEPT • BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS • FIGMA-STYLE UX
SchoolConnect Lite
A simple, beautiful single mobile app for school communication, homework, results and parent absence acknowledgement.



01 • EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
One app. Two role experiences. Four daily jobs.
SchoolConnect Lite is deliberately smaller than a full school ERP. It solves the high-frequency communication loop between teachers and parents using one role-aware mobile application. Teachers publish homework, result attachments and announcements; parents view these items for their linked children and acknowledge absence alerts.


Success criteria for the pilot
90%+ of published homework and announcements delivered to intended parents.
80%+ parent view rate within 24 hours for urgent announcements.
Absence acknowledgements visible to the school with timestamp and optional reason.
No cross-class or cross-child content exposure.
Teacher can publish a normal post in under 60 seconds.

02 • SCOPE & PRODUCT BOUNDARY
Simple by design, extensible by architecture

Business rules
A single account may have one or more roles, but the UI exposes only actions allowed for the active role.
A parent can see only children explicitly linked to that guardian account.
A teacher can publish only to classes/subjects assigned by the school.
Result attachments are private content; URLs must not be public or guessable.
Every post has created-by, audience, created-at, publish status, and attachment metadata.
Absence acknowledgement does not overwrite the official attendance record. It records parent response alongside it.
Deleted or corrected official content should retain an audit trail.

03 • USERS, PERSONAS & JOBS-TO-BE-DONE
Design around two people, not ten modules

Jobs-to-be-done
“When class ends, I want to publish homework with the right attachment in under a minute.”
“When results are ready, I want only the intended parents to access them.”
“When the school has an announcement, I want to know parents actually received/viewed it.”
“When my child is marked absent, I want to confirm I saw it and optionally tell the school why.”
“When I have two children, I want to switch children without logging out.”

09 • NOTIFICATIONS & COMMUNICATION POLICY
Useful without becoming noisy

Notification opens the exact child and exact content item.
Do not put result details or sensitive marks in push-notification text; use a generic “Result published” message.
Keep delivery status separate from view status and acknowledgement status.
Support school quiet hours for non-urgent posts in later iterations.

APPENDIX • ALIGNMENT WITH THE ATTACHED SCHOOL PLATFORM HLD
Narrow product, compatible foundation
This concept intentionally extracts only the communication, academic-content and attendance-follow-up slice from the larger AI School Platform high-level design supplied with this request. The proposed app remains compatible with that foundation rather than recreating the full ERP scope.

Why a single app matters

Control	Requirement
Guardian-child access	Parent requests are authorized using explicit guardian ↔ student relationship, not only class membership.
Teacher scope	Teacher publishing is restricted to assigned school/classes/subjects and permission level.
Tenant isolation	Every content and attendance request carries school context; cross-school access is denied by default.
Attachments	Store privately; malware/file validation; size/type limits; time-limited signed download/open URLs.
Results	Never expose a public attachment URL; log access; minimize sensitive details in notifications.
Authentication	Short-lived access tokens, refresh/session controls, device logout, optional staff MFA.
Audit	Capture publish/edit/archive, attendance corrections, acknowledgement, attachment access, permission changes.
Data retention	Define retention separately for posts, attachments, attendance, audit events and notifications.
Important product distinction
“Viewed” means the parent opened the item. “Acknowledged” is an explicit parent action. The system must never automatically convert a view into an acknowledgement.
